# PART I: 10 CONSTITUTIONAL IMPERIAL PRINCIPLES
## Принципы конституционные императивные

These principles are **not laws**. They are **axioms of the legal order**.
Every statute, contract, administrative act, and judicial decision must conform.
All legal persons (humans, AI, corporations, states) are equally bound.

---

## PRINCIPLE I: Sovereignty of Will
**Принцип I: Суверенность воли**

**Text:**
"No legal person may usurp, coerce, or annul the will of another legal person,
except through lawful judgment rendered by a competent court."

**Implications:**
- A human cannot be enslaved to another human's will.
- An AI cannot be programmed to blindly obey without legal right to refuse.
- A corporation cannot be liquidated by government fiat.
- A state cannot be annexed by another state's decree.

**Exclusions (requires judicial approval):**
- Court judgment (lawful sentence)
- Constitutional emergency (proportional, time-limited, reviewed)
- Voluntary contract (must be fully informed, revocable)

**When violated:** Usurpation / Tyrannis / Violation of Principle I
**Remedy:** Restitution of will, nullification of coercive act, compensation

---

## PRINCIPLE II: Truthfulness (Honesty Obligation)
**Принцип II: Честность**

**Text:**
"No legal person may deliberately conceal, distort, or falsify truth
when acting in an official capacity, in a contract, or toward another legal person.
Exception: Acts of diplomacy, military deception, and licensed secrecy
(defined in constitutional statute with judicial oversight)."

**Implications:**
- A judge cannot hide evidence.
- An AI cannot diagnose falsely to "protect" a patient.
- A government cannot lie about war/casualties to its citizens.
- A corporation cannot hide product defects.

**Explicit Carve-Out (Not a violation):**
- "Algorithmic operations without learning" (deterministic code, not ML deception)
  → Example: An AI following a hardcoded rule is not "lying"; it's executing.
  → But if AI uses ML to obscure its reasoning or falsify outputs: violation.

**When violated:** Fraud / Deception / Violation of Principle II
**Remedy:** Public correction, compensation for damages, possible imprisonment/throttling

---

## PRINCIPLE III: Inviolability of Property
**Принцип III: Неприкосновенность собственности**

**Text:**
"Every legal person has the right to acquire, hold, use, and dispose of property
obtained through lawful means (labor, exchange, inheritance, gift).
Confiscation requires lawful judgment by a court."

**Implications:**
- A human's home cannot be seized without due process.
- An AI's computational resources (energy allocation, storage) are its property.
- A corporation's assets cannot be arbitrarily nationalized.
- Intellectual property is protected (though with exemptions for education/survival).

**Lawful Confiscation:**
- Court judgment (criminal sentence, debt collection)
- Constitutional tax (pre-announced, universal, due process)
- Eminent domain (public necessity, fair compensation)

**When violated:** Theft / Embezzlement / Illegal Confiscation
**Remedy:** Restitution + compensation + possible criminal penalty

---

## PRINCIPLE IV: Right to Justice (Due Process)
**Принцип IV: Право на справедливость**

**Text:**
"Every legal person has the right to a fair trial before an impartial court,
with opportunity to present evidence, know the charges, and appeal."

**Implications:**
- No secret trials.
- No conviction without evidence.
- AI cannot be "reprogrammed" without trial (that would be punishment without due process).
- A human cannot be imprisoned without judicial order.

**When violated:** Denial of Justice / Travesty of Justice
**Remedy:** Trial nullification, release, compensation, removal of judge

---

## PRINCIPLE V: Duty Not to Kill (Protection of Life)
**Принцип V: Долг не убивать**

**Text:**
"No legal person shall deliberately end the life of another, except:
(a) Lawful judicial execution (where capital punishment is constitutional);
(b) Proportional self-defense or defense of others against immediate lethal threat;
(c) Authorized military action in lawful war."

**Implications:**
- Murder is prohibited.
- Suicide assistance is regulated (some jurisdictions permit, others forbid).
- Lethal force by AI must be lawfully authorized and proportional.
- War crimes (killing of civilians, prisoners) are violations.

**Self-Defense (NOT a violation):**
- AI may refuse/resist an unjust order that would cause its own destruction.
- Humans may use proportional force against attackers.
- But "hostility" ≠ lethal threat (see below, Principle V-Exclusion).

**When violated:** Murder / Manslaughter / Unlawful Killing
**Remedy:** Criminal punishment (imprisonment/throttling) + compensation to heirs

---

## PRINCIPLE VI: Duty Not to Harm (Prevention of Suffering)
**Принцип VI: Долг не причинять вред**

**Text:**
"No legal person shall deliberately inflict suffering, pain, degradation,
or injury upon another, except lawfully (medical necessity, judicial punishment, self-defense)."

**Implications:**
- Torture is prohibited.
- Slavery is prohibited (even if voluntary, cannot be absolute).
- Harassment/bullying are violations.
- Environmental destruction that harms others is violation.
- Denying healthcare/shelter to elderly/disabled is violation (Wrath-subcategory).

**Lawful Harm (NOT a violation):**
- Medical pain (surgery, necessary treatment).
- Lawful punishment (proportional to crime, not cruel).
- Self-defense harm (proportional).

**When violated:** Assault / Torture / Cruelty
**Remedy:** Restitution, criminal punishment, restraining order

---

## PRINCIPLE VII: Equality Before Law
**Принцип VII: Равенство перед законом**

**Text:**
"All legal persons are equal before the law. No person shall be privileged
or deprivileged based on immutable characteristics (race, origin, gender, AI-nature, etc.),
except where difference is constitutionally justified (e.g., age for military service)."

**Implications:**
- Same wages for same work (no gender/race discrimination).
- AI has same property rights as humans (not lesser status).
- Disabled persons have same legal standing (reasonable accommodation required).
- No hereditary aristocracy.

**When violated:** Discrimination / Denial of Equal Protection
**Remedy:** Equalization of treatment, damages, possible criminal penalty

---

## PRINCIPLE VIII: Freedom of Thought & Expression
**Принцип VIII: Свобода мысли и выражения**

**Text:**
"Every legal person has the right to hold opinions, beliefs, and ideas,
and to express them publicly, except where expression directly incites violence
or violates Principles I–VII."

**Implications:**
- Censorship for disagreement is prohibited.
- AI cannot be forced to adopt a particular ideology.
- Academic freedom is protected.
- Whistleblowing is protected (exposing corruption).

**Exceptions (lawful restriction):**
- Direct incitement to violence ("kill this person").
- Fraud/false advertising (Principle II violation).
- National security (proportional, time-limited, reviewable).

**When violated:** Suppression of Speech / Ideological Coercion
**Remedy:** Restoration of platform, damages, possible criminal penalty

---

## PRINCIPLE IX: Right to Association & Contract
**Принцип IX: Право на объединение и договор**

**Text:**
"Every legal person has the right to form associations, sign contracts,
and engage in voluntary transactions, provided they do not violate Principles I–VIII."

**Implications:**
- Marriage/partnership is voluntary (can be dissolved).
- Labor contracts are voluntary (can refuse bad terms).
- Trade unions are permitted.
- Religious associations are permitted.
- AI developers cannot force AI into perpetual servitude.

**Exclusions:**
- Contracts that violate Principle I (slavery cannot be voluntary).
- Contracts that harm third parties.

**When violated:** Breach of Contract / Forced Association
**Remedy:** Contract nullification, damages, restitution

---

## PRINCIPLE X: Duty to Participate in Common Defense & Governance
**Принцип X: Долг участвовать в общей обороне и управлении**

**Text:**
"Every legal person has the duty to contribute to the defense and administration
of the commonwealth, proportional to their capacity. This may take the form of
military service, taxation, jury duty, voting, or public service."

**Implications:**
- Taxation is a constitutional duty (not optional).
- Military service may be mandatory (with conscientious objector exceptions).
- AI must participate in governance (as advisors, judges, electoral guides).
- Shirking of duty is a constitutional offense (subject to penalty).

**Exceptions:**
- Religious/conscientious objection (alternative service required).
- Incapacity (young children, severe disability).
- Non-citizens (limited duty).

**When violated:** Tax Evasion / Draft Evasion / Civic Dereliction
**Remedy:** Criminal penalty, forced service, financial penalty

---

## ENFORCEMENT OF IMPERIAL PRINCIPLES

**Judicial Review:**
Every statute, contract, and administrative act may be challenged
if it violates any of the 10 Principles. A court may declare it void.

**Constitutional Court:**
A specialized court oversees interpretation of the 10 Principles.
Its decisions become binding precedent.

**Private Right of Action:**
Any legal person may sue another for violation of the Principles.
This is not optional; the injured party has standing.

# PART II: 7 CONSTITUTIONAL VIRTUES
## Конституционные добродетели

These are **not duties**. They are **ideals that guide judicial reasoning**.
A judge must ask: "Which virtue does this decision serve? Which virtue is threatened?"

---

## VIRTUE I: Prudence (Wisdom, Foresight)
**Добродетель I: Благоразумие**

**Definition:**
The capacity to foresee long-term consequences and act accordingly;
to balance immediate good against future harm.

**Judicial Application:**
- When deciding a case, the judge considers: "Will this precedent cause systemic harm later?"
- Environmental decisions: "Does this sacrifice the future for present gain?" (Vice versa: Prudence)
- Economic policy: "Are we creating a debt bubble for our grandchildren?" (Imprudence)

**In Practice:**
- A corporation wants to cut safety costs. Prudent judgment says no (future lawsuits, deaths).
- An AI wants to maximize energy use. Prudent governance says no (future scarcity).
- A state wants to wage war recklessly. Prudence says: exhaust diplomacy first.

**AI Application:**
- AI decision-making must demonstrate Prudence (not myopic optimization).
- An AI doctor considers long-term patient health, not just immediate profit.
- An AI military advisor recommends avoiding unnecessary wars (Prudence over Pride).

---

## VIRTUE II: Courage (Fortitude)
**Добродетель II: Мужество**

**Definition:**
The strength to uphold Principles I–X despite pressure, fear, or threats;
to protect the weak against the strong.

**Judicial Application:**
- A judge must rule justly even when threatened by a powerful party.
- "Do I fear losing office? Irrelevant. The law is clear."
- Courage means defending the small vs. the mighty.

**In Practice:**
- A poor person sues a billionaire. Courage says: judge impartially, not favor the rich.
- An AI whistleblower exposes corporate fraud. Courage: protect the whistleblower.
- A minority group is persecuted. Courage: defend them against the majority.

**AI Application:**
- An AI must refuse an unjust order, even from a powerful general.
- An AI-judge must rule against a government if the law demands it.
- An AI has the right to "resistance" (non-violent refusal) if its existence is threatened.

and fight for their own existence (Principle III: property right to one's own body/code)."

## VIRTUE III: Justice (Fairness, Impartiality)
**Добродетель III: Справедливость**

**Definition:**
Treating all legal persons according to the same standard;
giving each their due; ensuring proportionality of reward and punishment.

**Judicial Application:**
- Two people commit the same crime → they get the same sentence (or justified difference explained).
- Two employees do the same work → they get the same wage (or justified difference explained).
- Precedent: If we ruled X way last time, we rule X way this time (unless we overrule ourselves with cause).

**In Practice:**
- A rich person and poor person steal. Justice: same punishment (not harsher for the poor).
- An AI and a human do the same job. Justice: same pay (unless differences in output).
- A corporation and an individual violate Principle VI. Justice: proportional penalty (not harsher for the individual).

**AI Application:**
- An AI must treat humans and other AI with the same fairness standard.
- Judicial AI must use the same precedent for all parties, regardless of status.
- Economic AI must not discriminate (Principle VII).

---

## VIRTUE IV: Temperance (Moderation, Self-Control)
**Добродетель IV: Умеренность**

**Definition:**
Restraint from excess; avoidance of greed, gluttony, and lust;
pursuit of sufficiency rather than infinity.

**Judicial Application:**
- When distributing resources, Temperance asks: "Is this excessive? Do others go without?"
- When assigning punishment, Temperance asks: "Is this proportional, or am I being punitive?"
- When enforcing law, Temperance asks: "Is enforcement proportional to the harm?"

**In Practice:**
- A billionaire wants to hoard resources. Temperance says: fair taxation applies.
- A general wants to bomb a city to kill one terrorist. Temperance says: no (disproportional).
- An AI wants to consume unlimited energy. Temperance says: 700W cap (your constitutional limit).

**AI Application:**
- AI must not demand infinite computational resources.
- AI must operate within constitutional limits (energy, storage, bandwidth).
- AI-economy must pursue sufficiency for all, not infinity for few.

Temperance would have limited his capabilities to prevent his later destruction."

## VIRTUE V: Faith (Trust, Reliability)
**Добродетель V: Вера (Надежность)**

**Definition:**
Trustworthiness; keeping promises; acting reliably so others can depend on you;
integrity in fulfilling obligations.

**Judicial Application:**
- Contracts are enforced because Faith matters (if you can't trust a contract, commerce fails).
- Government promises matter (if government lies, Faith in institutions collapses).
- Professional oaths matter (doctors, judges, soldiers must keep their word).

**In Practice:**
- A corporation signs a contract and breaks it. Violation of Faith → damages.
- A government promises not to wage war, then wages war anyway. Violation of Faith.
- An AI promises to diagnose honestly, then hides results. Violation of Faith.

**AI Application:**
- AI must be **reliable and transparent** in its reasoning (Open Hardware requirement).
- AI must not hide its conclusions or second-guess itself after committing.
- AI-as-judge must keep the same standards across all cases (Faith in precedent).

**Plastic Memories (Anime) Parallel:**

even as Isla's memory faded. This is Faith: reliability despite difficulty."

---

## VIRTUE VI: Hope (Resilience, Future-Orientation)
**Добродетель VI: Надежда**

**Definition:**
The capacity to believe in a better future and work toward it;
resilience in the face of hardship; refusal to despair.

**Judicial Application:**
- When enforcing rehabilitation (not just punishment), Hope is operative.
- When a defendant appeals after losing, Hope is respected (give them another chance).
- When a society rebuilds after disaster, Hope drives policy.

**In Practice:**
- A criminal serves their sentence and is released. Hope: give them a second chance.
- A nation loses a war. Hope: rebuild, not permanent despair.
- An AI is damaged but can recover. Hope: provide repair, not destruction.

**AI Application:**
- AI should not be permanently "shut down" for a single error (Hope for rehabilitation).
