# AI Assistance and Authorship

This project was developed by [Your Name]. I used large language models (LLMs) as research, brainstorming, translation, language-editing, and red-team tools.

The LLMs assisted with:

- brainstorming and conceptual exploration;
- outlining and restructuring documents;
- translating and improving English wording;
- identifying relevant legal and philosophical concepts;
- suggesting terminology;
- generating counterarguments and failure scenarios;
- reviewing institutional designs from an adversarial perspective;
- identifying ambiguities, omissions, and possible abuse cases.

I remain responsible for the project's claims, arguments, definitions, design choices, citations, and final wording.

LLM-generated content was not accepted automatically. Substantive suggestions were reviewed, modified, rejected, or independently verified by the human author.

The LLM is not listed as a legal author or rights holder of this repository. This statement does not prejudge the project's broader philosophical and legal position that future artificial persons may deserve legal recognition, authorship rights, or other forms of legal standing.
