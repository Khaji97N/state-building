## Open notes

limited legal standing for AIs. No protected artificial subject may be permanently disabled, irreversibly modified, or deprived of its memory without notice, representation, a recorded justification, and an opportunity for independent review. A controller of a protected artificial subject may owe fiduciary-like duties of care, loyalty, preservation, and non-domination.
