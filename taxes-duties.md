# Monetary and Fiscal Affairs

## 1. Monetary Architecture (The Valuate System)
The State departs from fiat currency, introducing a system based on physical constants and temporal units to ensure absolute stability and transparency.

### 1.1. Valuate Potentials (Monetary Aggregates)
Replacing the traditional M0-M3 aggregates with **Valuate Potentials**, representing the capacity of the currency to expand and circulate.

| Standard Term | NCL Term (MEL) | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M0 (Base Money)** | **Valuate Potential** | The core monetary base and primary issuance. |
| **M1 (Liquid Money)** | **Extended Valuate Potential** | Core base plus highly liquid transactional assets. |
| **M2 + M3 (Broad Money)** | **Plenary Valuate Potential** | The total sum of all monetary assets within the system. |

### 1.2. The Valuate Standard
*   **Valuate Unit:** The basic unit of account.
*   **Valuate Basket:** A diversified reserve limited to a maximum of **5.00%** per single external currency (USD, EUR, JPY, etc.) to prevent dependency on any single foreign economy.
*   **Physical Anchors:** 
    *   **One Constitutional Pound** $\approx$ 720,720 Planck mass units.
    *   **One Constitutional Ounce** (Standardized subunit).
*   **Parallel Currency:** Integration of **Hayekian parallel money** (similar to the Danish krone's peg to the Euro), allowing for competitive and stable sub-currencies.

## 2. Fiscal Framework (The Tax Code)

### 2.1. The Constitutional Moratorium
To prevent bureaucratic bloating and economic suffocation, the State imposes a **Constitutional Moratorium on Taxation**:
*   **The Rule of 13:** A maximum of **13 types of taxes** is permitted.
*   **Strict Ban:** New taxes are prohibited; increases in existing taxes require a constitutional review.
*   **Debt Reduction:** Use of tax limitations as a primary tool for reducing national debt and loans.

### 2.2. The Tax Schedule (Example Rates)
Taxes are calculated based on **Base Temporal Units (BTU)** to align economic activity with time and energy.

### 2.3. Entrepreneurial Incentives
*   **Negative Income Tax (NIT):** Implementation of a Negative Income Tax for enterprises/businesses to stimulate growth.
*   **Emission-based Support:** NIT scaled based on emissions (e.g., specific rates per 1M OVE emission).

## 3. Customs and Duties (Cross-Border Flux)
To maintain a balance between open trade and national interest, a simplified duty system is applied:

*   **Unified Duty:** 5% flat rate for all cross-border movements.
*   **Import Duty:** 25% (Protection of internal production).
*   **Export Duty:** 30% (Prevention of strategic resource drain).
