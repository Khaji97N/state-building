# Constitutional Red-Team Review

## Red-Team Process

Each major proposal should undergo adversarial review before being treated as stable.

The reviewer should attempt to:

- identify hidden assumptions;
- construct abuse cases;
- find ways to capture institutional power;
- test minority protections;
- examine emergency powers;
- identify dependency on monopolies;
- test failure under resource scarcity;
- explore conflicts with other aspect cores;
- identify irreversible harms;
- propose alternative designs.

Red-team review is not intended to suppress disagreement. Its purpose is to improve institutional resilience and reveal risks before implementation.
